Hengwei Zhao (赵 恒伟)

Postdoctoral Research Associate

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at Texas A&M University.

My research focuses on weakly supervised geospatial machine learning, aiming to develop accurate and trustworthy algorithms for open Earth environments with limited annotation. In the future, I hope my research will contribute to global sustainable development and improve the quality of human life.
