Hengwei Zhao (赵恒伟)

Postdoctoral Research Associate

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at Texas A&M University.

My research focuses on weakly supervised remote sensing image analysis, aiming to develop accurate and trustworthy algorithms for open Earth environments under limited supervision. In the future, I hope my research will contribute to global sustainable development and improve the quality of human life.

Perhaps intelligence, after all, is something that can be computed.
